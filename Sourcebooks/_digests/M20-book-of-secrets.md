# Digest: Mage: The Ascension 20th — The Book of Secrets

> Scan-fast reference for *The Book of Secrets: An Expansive Companion for Mage 20th Anniversary* (Onyx Path, 2017). This book **adds character options and optional subsystems** to M20; it does **not** replace core resolution, the condition track, or base advancement. Treat everything here as opt-in. Cite this digest in `Game/system.md` when live.

## System & source
- **Game & line:** Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary (M20)
- **Source:** *The Book of Secrets*. This digest covers Ch. One "Heroic Traits" (Archetypes, expanded Abilities, Merits & Flaws) and Ch. One/Two combat + Certámen (pp. 13–115). Later chapters (Resonance pp. 128–138, Wonders pp. 139–165, InfoTech/Hacking pp. 116–127, Expanded Instruments/Practices Ch. Three) exist but are not digested here — digest on first need.
- **Dice subcommand:** `m20` (pass to `Tools/dice.py`).
- **Nature:** an **expansion**. Every Trait and rule below is an *optional add-on* the Storyteller may allow or disallow. See **Overrides** at the bottom — almost nothing here changes a default; it adds choices.

## Character options

### Archetypes / Natures (pp. 14–17)
Expands the M20 core list (M20 pp. 250, 267–273) with these new Natures/Demeanors. Each regains **Willpower** on its trigger:
- **Architect** — builds things that endure; regain WP on creating/providing something of lasting value.
- **Artist** — reshapes reality as a canvas; regain WP when art communicates sublime Truth to its audience.
- **Bon Vivant** — chases joy/experience; regain WP when you have a truly awesome time and bring others along.
- **Caregiver** — there when people need you; regain WP on protecting/comforting/nurturing someone.
- **Conformist** — the reliable team player; regain WP when you support a plan you backed up.
- **Director** — takes charge, lays down order; regain WP when you lead a group through a challenging task.
- **Entertainer** — brightens/illuminates; regain WP when your work meaningfully changes someone's life.
- **Guardian** — shields the weak; regain WP when you save a weaker party from assured destruction.
- **Heretic** — overturns sacred cows; regain WP when you challenge a held conviction and change minds.
- **Mentor** — teaches/invests in students; regain WP when your guidance lets a pupil reach something new.
- **Romantic** — seeks beauty, even tragic; regain WP when you throw yourself into a gloriously ruinous affair.
- **Tycoon** — money/influence as the game; regain WP when a long-shot plan pays off in grand style (via influence/love/achievement, not mere cash).
- **Vigilante** — justice beyond the law; regain WP when you inflict major suffering on a target larger than one person (org, conspiracy).
- **Zealot** — extreme belief (flipside of Heretic); regain WP tied to advancing the cause (per the Zealot's chosen creed).

#### Supernatural Merits (pp. 68–79) — fuller gloss; these are the story-load-bearing ones
- **Burning Aura (1)** — aura blazes; +2 diff to hide it, Resonance/Synergy each get a free dot; some entities react well, some treat you as a meal.
- **Green Thumb (1)** — -2 diff non-Arete and -1 Arete rolls when working with plant matter.
- **Bardic Gift (2)** — -2 diff on Art rolls; creations glow with Truth even on a failure.
- **Circumspect Avatar (2)** — a "silent" Avatar that drops mundane hints instead of overt Seekings.
- **Faerie Affinity (2)** — -2 diff dealing with changelings; they reciprocate. Forbidden to technomancers.
- **Medium (2)** — talk to ghosts without Spirit; Perception+Awareness senses them; -2 diff social with the dead.
- **Unaging (2)** — body frozen at current age (not immortality — can still be killed).
- **Celestial Affinity (3, +3/extra category)** — -2 diff summon/negotiate one spirit type (does NOT help binding/warding); +2 dice to Arete to summon them.
- **Cloak of the Seasons (3)** — immune to weather extremes.
- **Cyclic Magick (3, Merit/Flaw)** — Arts tied to a cycle: peak gives up to **-3** casting diff, nadir up to **+3**; tapers across the cycle.
- **Danger Sense (3)** — ST makes secret Perception+Awareness to warn of impending harm; diff by immediacy (6 bullet / 10 distant plot).
- **Hands of Daedalus (3)** — -2 diff to craft/repair/modify machines; hardware acts as personalized instruments.
- **Lucky (3)** — reroll up to **3 dice per story** (each redo once).
- **Manifest Avatar (3)** — Avatar is a real, daily-present character (ST-run, with Allies-like solidity).
- **Mark of Favor (3)** — a god-form has claimed you; -2 diff related socials, may grant minor ST-whim powers.
- **Natural Channel (3)** — -2 diff to penetrate the Gauntlet.
- **Natural Shapeshifter (3)** — -2 diff to self-shapeshifting (still needs Life Sphere); no risk of losing self.
- **Oracular Ability (3)** — read omens: Perception+Awareness (diff 7) to spot a message, then Perception+Esoterica/Occult to interpret (diff 6–9 by randomness).
- **Parlor Trick (3, up to ×3)** — one specific non-combat, Sleeper-explainable trick with **no casting roll** (limited to Spheres 1–3, nothing vulgar, no weapons).
- **Spirit Magnet (3–7, Merit/Flaw)** — spirits cluster around you; Merit = benign, Flaw = Banes/demons; value scales with how many/how potent.
- **Spirit Mentor (3)** — a spirit teaches you (not Sphere magick, but Abilities/insight); ST-run character.
- **Supernatural Companion (3)** — a Night-Folk friend (vampire/changeling/etc.); Allies-like but cuts both ways.
- **Stormwarden/Quantum Voyager (3/5, see M20 p. 643)**.
- **Deathwalker (4)** — step into the Dead Lands via Spirit 3; pass the Low Umbra without the Avatar Storm.
- **Fae Blood (4)** — kinain; low Banality (≤4), permanently enchanted. Forbidden to technomancers.
- **Shapechanger Kin (4)** — distant were-blood; immune to Delirium, easier Umbral travel; their enemies are yours.
- **Twin Souls (4)** — your Avatar has a literal soulmate twin in another body; shared Nature/Essence; collaborative casting and shared Paradox if both are mages; twin's death = Willpower roll or trauma.
- **Umbral Affinity (4, see M20 p. 644)**.
- **Unbondable (4)** — immune to vampiric blood bond; **+2 Willpower to resist Presence/Dominate/Chicanery-style domination**. (ST may double cost in vampire-heavy chronicles.)
- **Ghoul (5)** — you've drunk vitae: +1 auto success on Strength rolls, +1 die hand-to-hand damage, slowed aging; but addictive, needs vitae monthly, gains corrupt Resonance/Echoes. **See sidebar "Mages, the Blood Bond, and the Effects of Vitae" (p. 76)** — a ghouled mage risks blood-bond (Dominate at -2 diff to resist), Addiction Flaw + Deranged, and as an optional rule loses 1 Avatar/Arete dot per year ghouled.
- **Clear Sighted (5)** — Perception+Awareness to see through *deception* powers (illusions, Obfuscate, faerie cantrips, Sphere glamours); -3 diff vs disguises; does NOT pierce Presence/Dominate.
- **"Immortal" (5/7)** — age slowly and very hard to kill; **requires a special doom** (one thing that *will* kill you for good). 5pt = slow aging + recover from most injuries/illness; 7pt = immune to death itself save the doom or total bodily obliteration.
- **Inner Knight (5)** — for skeptics; in "hope is lost" crises, access Dream 5 traits, +5 temp Willpower, recall past-life knowledge (lasts the scene).
- **Legendary Attributes (5)** — buy one Attribute up to **6 dots**, plus a heroic capability tied to it (ST approval, kicks in at 6).
- **Powerful Ally (5–8)** — like Supernatural Companion but far more potent (Prince, Garou elder, etc.); scale by power.
- **Shattered Avatar (5)** — Avatar broken; lets you raise Avatar Background post-creation (caps at 5) by reuniting the fragments — big story-hook engine.
- **Spark of Life (5)** — heal lethal as bashing, aggravated as one level milder; heal/soothe others at -2 diff; bashing heals in an hour.
- **Guardian Angel (6)** — a powerful entity protects you at the ST's whim; potentially with a sinister price.
- **Nine Lives (6)** — cheat death up to nine times: a death-dealing roll must be re-rolled; failure burns one "life." Can't combine with Spark of Life.

- **Avatar Companion (7)** — a companion who follows you across incarnations, knows your reincarnated self; ST-run.
- **Dual Affiliation (7)** — trained in two Awakened groups; use the Affinity Sphere XP rate for one Sphere per group.
- **Nephilim/Laham (7)** — High-Umbral/demon-blooded heritage: Quintessence 7 (refills nightly), 3 dots Resonance (or Echoes Flaw), -1 diff to Mind/Spirit vs High Umbra, +2 dice to bargain with High Umbral entities, +2 diff to resist Hermetic/Abrahamic high rituals; **must take ≥3 pts Physical Flaws** (don't count vs limit).

## Expanded rules (Chapter Two, pp. 101–115) — all OPTIONAL

### Knocking people unconscious (p. 102)
Optional rule: bashing damage that drives a target to **Incapacitated** can render them unconscious instead of pulping them (lasts until they recover to Crippled+Stunned, then Wounded). A successful **Knockout Blow** stunt decks for 1 turn/success. Mind 2/3 sleep spells, Life 3 (no brain damage; target rolls Willpower diff 6 to resist), or Life 4 transform are the magickal routes.

### Certámen — classic wizard duels (pp. 112–114, expanding M20 pp. 432–434)
A formal "gentlemen's duel" of magick. Basics (the **Old Form Certámen**) are in M20 pp. 432–434; this book adds *optional tactics*. Terminology: lowercase **sphere** = the floating dueling weapons/shield/reserves; capital **Sphere** = the nine arts. A duelist conjures glowing spheres with **Arete**, shaping a **Gladius** (attack weapon), an **Aegis** (shield), a **Locus** (Quintessence pool), plus reserve spheres.

How a duel runs with these tactics:
- **Intimidation (p. 112):** a combatant facing an opponent with ≥3 more glowing spheres must roll Willpower (diff 4 +1/extra sphere) or auto-lose initiative the first turn (botch = always second).
- **Blocking:** defender rolls Wits + Aegis Sphere vs (attacker's Gladius Sphere +3) to fully deflect; can use Multiple Actions or Time 3 to block at full pool.
- **Botching:** loses both Gladius and Aegis (must Rearm).
- **Disarming:** attack roll diff 9 vs target Gladius/Aegis; 1 succ disrupts it 1 turn, 4+ destroys it.
- **Rearming:** reform from a reserve sphere (1 turn) or conjure new (2 turns); unarmed/unprotected meanwhile.
- **Locus Dodge:** defender moves the Locus — Wits+Arete (diff 6), each success cancels one of attacker's successes (full action).
- **Switching Spheres:** swap which Sphere is Gladius/Aegis/reserve; free normally, but mid-match under duress roll Wits+Arete or Willpower (diff 7) — fail = a free attack to the opponent.
- **Surprise Intimidation, Refueling (Prime 2 to pull Tass into the Locus, diff 6 — refueling from a Node is bad form), Psychological Warfare (Social + Ability, -1 to defender's next roll/success), Reshaping the Spheres (Wits+Arete, diff 5 winning / 7 losing), Tapping a Reserve, To the Pain (grudge match: +3 Quint per permanent Willpower dot; fights to unconsciousness).**
- **Optional "Special Sphere Effects" (p. 113):** each Sphere grants a unique combat/reserve edge in certámen — e.g. **Correspondence (reserve):** +1 attack-or-defense die per dot; **Entropy (Gladius):** +1 auto success but burns Quint; **Forces (Gladius):** bonus attack die per 10 rolled (if ≥1 ten); **Life (Aegis):** +1 soak die per dot; **Matter:** +1 attack/defense die per dot; **Mind (Gladius/Aegis):** +1 diff to hit/block per dot; **Prime (reserve):** -1 to attack+defense diff per rank (split, max -3); **Spirit (Gladius):** +1 auto Quint drain per 10 rolled; **Time (reserve):** spend 1 Quint per dot-over-1 for an extra action.

## Overrides
This is an **expansion**; it overrides almost nothing by default. Distinguish:

**Drop-in additions (just add; no default changed):**
- New Archetypes/Natures (use exactly like core Natures).
- All expanded Secondary Abilities (normal dot-rated Abilities feeding standard pools).
- The entire Merits & Flaws catalog (optional; ST approves each).
- All combat stunts, advanced weapon maneuvers, the Mexican Standoff, and the Certámen tactics — added on top of M20 Ch. Six/Nine/Ten combat.

**Optional toggles the ST must explicitly switch ON, that *do* change a baseline when adopted:**
- **Sphere XP costs** are altered *only for the specific Merits* Sphere Natural (70%/round up) and Affinity-rate Merits (Dual Affiliation, etc.) per the table on p. 79 — not a global advancement change.
- **Knock-unconscious rule** (p. 102) — changes how Incapacitating bashing resolves *if adopted*.
- **Hollywood Reality** (p. 103) — relaxes vulgar/coincidental judgment in cinematic scenes *if adopted*.
- **Higher Flaw cap of 13** (p. 35) — replaces the default 7-pt Flaw ceiling *if adopted*.
- **"What Have I Done?"** (p. 110) — adds a psychological-cost layer to violence *if adopted*.
- **Certámen Special Sphere Effects / To the Pain** (pp. 113–114) — only inside certámen, only if used.

**Unchanged:** core d10 resolution, difficulty/success/botch rules, the Health/condition track, the d6 oracle, base advancement, and Paradox/vulgar-coincidental rules all stand as in CLAUDE.md and M20 core unless a toggle above is turned on. When unsure, treat any Book-of-Secrets material as additive and optional.
