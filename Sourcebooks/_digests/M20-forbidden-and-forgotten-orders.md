# Digest: Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary — Forbidden and Forgotten Orders

> A scan-fast roster of the **Disparate Crafts** — the independent and lesser-known mage factions — for offering as PC/NPC options at character creation. This is an **expansion** of available factions, not a rules replacement. See **Overrides** at the bottom: it changes nothing in core resolution, the condition track, or XP.

## System & source
- **Game & line:** Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary (M20)
- **Source:** *Forbidden and Forgotten Orders* (Onyx Path / Paradox, 2024). Covers the eleven Crafts of the **Disparate Alliance** plus their politics. Intro pp. 5–13; Ch.1 (Forerunners) pp. 15–51; Ch.2 (Middlers) pp. 53–89; Ch.3 (Outsiders) pp. 91–116.
- **Dice subcommand:** `m20` (pass to `Tools/dice.py`).
- **What it is:** A character/faction sourcebook. Every Craft is a full mage faction usable as a PC's affiliation (the way a Tradition or Convention is), with its own paradigm, favored Spheres/foci, faction Merits/Flaws, and signature rotes. Mages use ordinary M20 Spheres/Arete/Paradox; what changes is the *flavor and faction-specific Traits*, not the engine.

## The Disparate Alliance — overview (pp. 5–13)
The Disparates are the Crafts that withdrew from (or were excluded from) the Council of Nine Traditions and the Technocracy. Long isolated and self-reliant, they've recently formed an uneasy **Alliance** for survival. The book sorts them into three loose tiers by commitment to the Alliance:
- **Forerunners** (most invested, want to lead): Ngoma, Templar Knights, Taftâni, Hollow Ones.
- **Middlers** (hopeful but wary, old blood feuds): Ahl-i-Batin, Kahu (Kopa Loei), Sisters of Hippolyta, Wu Lung.
- **Outsiders** (fringe, fiercely self-reliant): Bata'a, Solificati (Children of Knowledge), Orphans.

All are broadly **anti-Technocracy**; their stance toward the Traditions ranges from rival to estranged ex-member. Most reject the label "Disparate"/"Orphan" as an outsider's slur.

## CHAPTER 1 — THE FORERUNNERS

### Ngoma (pp. 16–25)
- **Concept:** Ancient African wizard-priests, the **Ngo-Ami**, heirs of Kush/Khermit (Egypt/Nubia) traditions; scholar-mages who bargain with **the Ancestors** (powerful spirits/kartek of the Unseen Worlds). Library/School-based, diaspora now global.
- **Paradigm/practice:** Ancestor-veneration, dance/chant/drumming ritual magick, spirit-bargaining; theme of reclaiming stolen heritage ("New Kush Initiative").
- **Favored Spheres/foci:** Strong in **Spirit and Prime** (Ancestor work). Foci: drum and rhythm, chant, dance, the Key Language *Ufungoto*.
- **Politics:** See themselves as natural Alliance leaders (the "shepherds"). Bitter rivalry with the **Order of Hermes**, who they say stole their teachings ("House Ngoma"). Warmest bond is with the **Bata'a** (the diaspora cousins — the "Ibeji/Twin Crafts"). Internal factions: **Lightbringers** (pro-Alliance), **Curators** (insular isolationists), **Meroe Priesthood** (pro-Tradition outreach).
- **Hook:** A Warrior Scholar, Librarian, or Griot drawn into Alliance politics or hunting looted artifacts.
- **Mechanics:** *Aid From the Ancestors* (3pt Supernatural Merit) — an Ancestor reduces Spirit-Bargaining difficulty by −2 (−1 more with the focus ritual). *Honor and Thanks* (focus ritual) — a small ritual gesture lowers difficulty by −1 on Spirit or Prime actions.

### Templar Knights / Knights of the Temple of Solomon (pp. 26–34)
- **Concept:** Christian holy warrior-mages descended from the historical Knights Templar; faith-fueled crusaders preparing for the Apocalypse, protecting the innocent and hunting evil. Recently reformed under their first female Grand Master, **Charlotte de la Roux**, opening to women and non-Christian denominations.
- **Paradigm/practice:** Militant faith ("A World of Gods and Monsters," "It's All Good — Have Faith and One-Way Trip to Oblivion"); High Ritual + Faith magick, alchemy of the **Divine Fire**.
- **Favored Spheres/foci:** **Forces, Life, Mind, Prime** (the "Earthly emanations of God"). Foci: bibles, hymnals, crosses, swords (especially **Orichalcum** blades). Decentralized cell structure under the Grand Master.
- **Politics:** Hated enemies of the **Nephandi** (seen as the Antichrist) and the **Technocracy**. Allies with the **Celestial Chorus** (uneasily) and the **Ahl-i-Batin** (old Crusade foes turned comrades). Patriarchal past makes other Crafts (esp. Sisters of Hippolyta) skeptical.
- **Hook:** A Perfect Knight proving herself, a "Templar banker" (the Money) running the Order's finances, or a diplomat-confessor (A Light in the Dark).
- **Mechanics:** Unique access to **Primium** — they call it **Orichalcum/"Christ's Blood"**; mechanically identical to Primium (countermagick alloy, can harm shapeshifters/undead, hold enchantments). Backgrounds: *Orichalcum Blade* (1–5), *Vatican Connections* (1–5, like specialized Contacts to the Holy See/Society of Leopold), *Knight-Captain* (1–5, a squad of non-Awakened soldiers; optional 2–10 for True Faith members), *Solomon Codes* (1–2pt Resources Merit — Templar-mission-only funds).

### Taftâni (pp. 35–42)
- **Concept:** The **Weavers** — ancient Persian/Arabian willworkers who weave **Asha** (Truth) into reality, masters of djinn and bold, unsubtle magick. Nearly extinct, now re-emerging (notably in Dubai) and embracing modern tech/social media.
- **Paradigm/practice:** "Reality is what you make it" — proud, vulgar magick; commanding spirits and djinn; theme of openly showing Sleepers the truth.
- **Favored Spheres/foci:** **Forces, Prime, Spirit** for the warrior **Zaotar**; **Entropy, Mind, Time** for the diviner **Kahin**. Foci: pottery/clay, weaving, crystal balls, smartphones, the **djinn bottle**.
- **Politics:** Complex frenemy history with the **Ahl-i-Batin** (subtle vs. blatant — opposite philosophies, old bloodshed). Disdain the **Order of Hermes** ("children compared to us"); respect the **Sons of Ether** as fellow dreamers. Their blatancy worries cautious Alliance partners.
- **Hook:** A Kahin Sage with no apprentice, a globe-trotting "Star of the Socials" influencer-mage, or a Zaotar Fighter shielding the vulnerable.
- **Mechanics:** *Paradox Mitigation* (3pt Supernatural Merit) — first vulgar spell per scene generates no Paradox; acquires Paradox Flaws one level later than normal. *Djinn Expertise* (3pt Supernatural Merit) — +1 die to Manipulation/Perception when dealing with djinn. *Obvious Resonance* (2pt Supernatural Flaw) — resonance lingers (days = Arete), letting trackers follow. *Djinn Bottle* (focus item). Signature rotes: *Devouring Gullet of Flame* (Forces/Prime), *Groundswell* (Forces/Matter), *Imbue Item* (Matter/Prime — animate a created object), *Sha'ir's Sentence* (Entropy/Life/Mind — bind a fate-altering nickname).

### Hollow Ones (pp. 43–51)
- **Concept:** The **Darklings** — gothic, romantic, anti-authority orphan-mages who belong to no Tradition or Convention. Eclectic chaos-mages who steal/synthesize any tradition's lore into a personal "magick quilt," united by style, outsider identity, and self-actualizing belief.
- **Paradigm/practice:** Chaos Magick ethic — "anything works if you believe it and it has meaning"; "Death and Beauty" paradigms (Everything is Chaos/Illusion/a Mistake, but passion endures). Romance and story are the engine of their willworking.
- **Favored Spheres/foci:** No fixed affinity (true eclectics). Foci: gothic clothing/trinkets, music, poetry, art, ritual borrowed from anywhere. Organized loosely in **cliques** (study groups), not chantries.
- **Politics:** The unofficial **"Tenth Tradition"** — dislike everyone but get along with fellow outsiders. Natural allies of **Verbena**, **Cult of Ecstasy**, **Sons of Ether**, and even vampires/werewolves/fae. Internal flavors: Councilors (pro-recognition), Revolutionaries, Soundwave Masters (rave/DJ mages), Activists, Moles, Masters.
- **Hook:** A Byronic Knight (Awakened resisting the Technocracy), a Harlem Connection activist, or Media Royalty (social-media mage).
- **Mechanics:** Chaos Magick (instruments rule — anything evocative is a valid focus). *Divergent Protocols* (2pt Supernatural Merit — access Technocracy-style Procedures/cybernetics with gear). *Anarchist Braggart* (1pt Social Flaw — Wits+Empathy to hold your tongue). *Riding the Rails* (Correspondence 3 / Time 3 rote — the "Railroad" teleport network between Hollow sanctum doors).

## CHAPTER 2 — THE MIDDLERS
*(All carry old, bloody blood-feud histories; newly Awakened members are warned the Alliance is "a pit of vipers.")*

### Ahl-i-Batin (pp. 54–62)
- **Concept:** The **Subtle Ones** — ascetic Persian/Arab/North-African mystics seeking **Unity** (oneness with the Divine). Scholars, astrologers, viziers, diplomats. Former Tradition (left the Council of Nine). Maintain the global **Web of Faith** (a network of Nodes/conduits, originally aqueducts).
- **Paradigm/practice:** Subtlety and patience; "all things are part of a united whole." Work slowly, invisibly, through symbol and pattern. Tied to (but not the same as) Islam/Judaism scholarship. Ascetic discipline. Fight infernalists (Nephandi) across millennia; coined the role of **Qutb** ("Judge").
- **Favored Spheres/foci:** **Correspondence and Mind** above all (space and thought); **Entropy is Sphere Inept** (philosophically opposed to dissolution). Foci: trained mind, geometric patterns, water, small ritual objects.
- **Politics:** Tense reconciliation with the **Taftâni** (they "let them be the easy target" historically — owe them an apology). Some migrated to the **Order of Hermes** when they left the Council. Watchful for Nephandic infiltration; cautiously hopeful about the Alliance.
- **Hook:** A Media Monitor fighting misinformation, a Global Citizen funding the Web, or a Self-Reliant Disciple — untrained, reaching for the Divine through hardship.
- **Mechanics:** Rotes: *Communion* (Mind 3/Time 3, +Corr 2 — telepathic shared-mind collaboration), *Fana* (Corr 3/Mind 3 — sublimate the self into another's senses), *Out of Harm's Way* (Corr 3 — desync a target from physical contact), *Patterning* (Mind 2 — program behavioral triggers over time), *The Slopes of Mount Qaf* (Corr 4/Mind 3/Forces 2 — collocate in Web and material world). Merits: *Occultation* (3pt — choose when Arcane applies), *Unity of Purpose* (2pt Social — −1 diff cooperating with a shared-paradigm mage, −2 with another Batini), *Web of Faith* (1–5pt Supernatural — once/session convert to Chantry/Dream/Destiny/Node dots for a scene). Flaws: *Exposed* (1/3pt Social), *Sphere Inept: Entropy*.

### Kahu / Kopa Loei (pp. 63–71)
- **Concept:** Native Hawaiian/Polynesian guardian-priests (**kahuna**), keepers of **mana** (the essence of all life) and the land's sacred sites (heiau). Wounded by colonization; now in a cultural renaissance and reaching out to the Alliance after the Avatar Storm severed their spirit-voyaging.
- **Paradigm/practice:** "Creation is Innately Divine and Alive," "Bring Back the Golden Age," "All Power Comes from God(s)," "We're All God(s) in Disguise." Practices: Faith, High Ritual, Shamanism, Elementalism, God-Bonding, Mediumship. Divided into 10 specialties (e.g. **Oracles** = kilokilo/divination, **Wayfinders** = ho'okele-moana/navigation+spirits, **Flametenders** = ho'omanamana/imbuing objects).
- **Favored Spheres/foci:** No formal Sphere belief — can use **any** force in nature. Surfers favor Entropy/Forces/Time; crafters favor Prime/Forces/Matter; navigators Correspondence/Time. Foci: chant (mele), dance, ancestral heirlooms, shells, driftwood, ivory (avoid taboo lava rock, human bone, sand).
- **Politics:** Kinship with the **Bata'a** (shared diaspora gods, anti-colonial struggle) and the **Dreamspeakers/Kha'vadi** (respected cousins). Wary of the **Celestial Chorus** (missionary grudge) and **Technocracy** (the colonizers). Distrust the **Chakravanti/Euthanatos**.
- **Hook:** An Activist fighting overtourism, a Performer dancer/musician spreading the culture, or a Voyager following a vanished elder's quest.
- **Mechanics:** *Aumakua* (7pt Supernatural Merit — an ancestral/nature spirit guide, both Avatar Companion and Spirit Mentor; teaches Abilities/Rotes, not Spheres). *Kapu* (4–5pt Psychological Flaw — traditionalist taboos that cripple mana use if broken: e.g. cannot take lava rock without permission, cannot eat near a different gender, cannot touch a corpse). Rotes: *Pule Pale* (Entropy 2 — protective chant), *Accelerate the Current* (Forces 3 — ignore vehicle speed penalties), *Searching for the Lost Path* (Corr 2/Time 2 — retrace another's journey; uses a departure-time/success/duration table).

### Sisters of Hippolyta / Hippolytae (pp. 72–80)
- **Concept:** Women-only Craft of warrior-mystics, spiritual heirs of the Amazons, dedicated to fighting patriarchy and protecting women. Battle-oriented but purposeful; explicitly **trans-inclusive and anti-racist** (their words: "sisters, not cisters"; no TERFs). Nearly wiped out at the 1922 Smyrna catastrophe ("Themiscyra"); now organized into **conclaves** governed by an elected committee of seven (**epitropi**).
- **Paradigm/practice:** Ritual-heavy shamanistic magick + martial arts; Artemis worship, festivals (Elaphebolia, Amarysia). Paradigms: "Creation is Innately Divine and Alive," "It's All Good — Have Faith!," "Might Is Right," "Ancient Wisdom is the Key." Practices: elementalism (Tunisian), god-bonding/invigoration (Greek).
- **Favored Spheres/foci:** Affinity **Life, Spirit, or Mind**. Foci: blood/body fluids, sensuality, bones, weapons, brews, herbs, plants. Conclaves: Themiscyra (Greece, elders), Denver (most powerful), Paris, Vancouver (most progressive).
- **Politics:** Skeptical of the **Traditions** (resentful they abandoned them at Themiscyra) and the **Technocracy**. Warm toward the **Kahu** and **Bata'a**; cautiously watching the reformed **Templars** ("let them walk the walk"). Long, fraught history with the **Verbena**.
- **Hook:** A Social Justice Witch (campus feminist newly Awakened), a Mother of the House (trans ballroom-scene mentor), an Amazon Next Door (veteran social-worker prodigy).
- **Mechanics:** *Legacy* (2pt Social Merit — born into a Sister bloodline: −2 Social diff with Craft, −1 with other Awakened). *Blessing of Artemis* (3pt Physical Merit — born on Elaphebolia: +2 dice to Dexterity for a scene, once/scene). *Vessel of Themis* (2/4pt Supernatural Flaw — birthmark + disturbing visions of other women's suffering; *potentially triggering — discuss lines/veils*). Rotes: *Mark of the Huntress* (Life 3/Mind 2/Prime 2 — mark a predator as dangerous), *Break the Bonds* (Spirit 3 — nymphs free a bound Sister), *One for All* (Corr 2/Life 3/Prime 2 — blood-bread ritual divides pain/wounds among Sisters).

### Wu Lung (pp. 81–89)
- **Concept:** Chinese dragon-sorcerers ("**Dragon Witches**"), an ancient secret society once advisors to emperors; modern branches in **Hong Kong, Taiwan, mainland China**. Split into two sects: the young, progressive, democratic **Yellow Dragons** (Wong Lung — pro-people, the PC default) vs. the older, establishment **Azure Dragons** (serve the elites).
- **Paradigm/practice:** Chinese folk magick rooted in **the Five Arts** (xian/mysticism, medicine, astrology, geomancy/feng shui, palm-reading/divination); wuxing (five elements) and bagua (eight signs). Paradigms: "A Mechanistic Cosmos," "Divine Order and Earthly Chaos," "Ancient Wisdom is the Key." Practices: weird science, alchemy, medicine work, High Ritual, invigoration, god-bonding.
- **Favored Spheres/foci:** Astrology/geomancy mages favor **Correspondence and Prime**; god-bonders favor **Spirit**. Foci: geomancy/astrology charts, potions/brews, **Fu** (paper talismans — modern mages use sticky notes), **Tan** (makeshift altar), peachwood sword, incense/food offerings.
- **Politics:** Allied with the **Akashic Brotherhood** (co-evolved Eastern roots). Anti-**Technocracy** (Chinese-government technomancers), but skeptical the **Traditions** can speak for all. Kinship with the **Kahu** (Hong Kong's Chinese diaspora ties).
- **Hook:** A Po Po (grandma, longtime pro-people mage), a Young Dragon (Catholic-schooled youth who found her roots), or a Wayward Son torn between Azure-Dragon family and Yellow-Dragon conscience.
- **Mechanics:** Zi Wei Dou Shu (Purple Star astrology) Merits/Flaws: *Wenqu* (2pt Social — +2 dice artistic Expression), *Wuqu* (2pt Resources — treat Resources as 2 higher once/story), *Zuofu* (2pt Social — Storyteller introduces a helpful NPC once/session), *Dijie* (2pt Resources Flaw — Resources never above 1), *Qingyang* (1pt Social Flaw — −2 first-impression dice). Rotes: *Godly Possession: Hua Tuo* (Spirit 2 — +2 Medicine), *Godly Possession: Guan Yu* (Spirit 3 — upgrade unarmed/melee damage), *Qimen Dunjia* (Correspondence 3 — battlefield positioning ±1 diff). Specialty items: peachwood sword (spend Willpower → aggravated for a scene), Fu, Tan (ritual at −1 diff or 2 banked successes).

## CHAPTER 3 — THE OUTSIDERS

### Bata'a (pp. 92–101)
- **Concept:** African-diaspora mages of the Americas/Caribbean — **the Seven Clans Made One**, descendants of seven West/Central African peoples enslaved in the New World. Magick of **resistance and liberation**, serving **les Mystères** (the Loa/Orishas/Ancestors). Frame themselves as "the Unknown Revolutionaries." Voiced as a hip-hop/podcast mixtape ("Ring Shout").
- **Paradigm/practice:** Vodou/Santería/Candomblé/Hoodoo-derived syncretic magick; offerings, possession (riding the Loa), rootwork. Theme: Knowledge → Acceptance → Service = Liberation. Organized in small chosen-family cells called **Creyente** (3–7 members), led by an elder (Houngan/Santero, etc.); members carry dual names.
- **Favored Spheres/foci — one per Clan:** **Kongo** (Prime, astrology), **Igbo** (Entropy, augury), **Akan** (Forces, cleromancy/cowries), **Mande** (Life, rootwork/healing+poison), **Fon** (Correspondence, transmission/messengers), **Ewe** (Mind, clairvoyance/keepers of lore), **Yoruba** (Spirit, possession/the warrior path). Foci: drums, song, dance, vévés, offerings (liquor, food, tobacco), the **Mojo Bag**.
- **Politics:** Closest kinship with the **Ngoma** (the "Twin/Ibeji Crafts" — "Brotherman from the Motherland") and the **Orphans** ("brothers from other mothers"). Will ally with **Verbena** or **Taftâni** situationally. **Hate the Templar Knights** ("Fuck. The. Templar. Knights!" — the Oppressor's lapdogs). Anti-Technocracy ("the Oppressor").
- **Hook:** The Grand Dame (ageless Mande matriarch — *advanced, talk to ST*), the Ewe Caregiver (Grammy-winning artist guarding the lore), the Yoruba Judge (ex-con possessed by Shango, "Queen of the Hood").
- **Mechanics:** *Monter a Chwal / Riding the Horse* — signature possession magick (mostly Yoruba); a Chwal is bonded to les Mystères via a Ring Shout ritual. Instruments: *Sèvitè* (7pt Background, req. Arete 5 — a conjure-staff: +3 dice to affinity Sphere, +4 attack as a weapon), *Mojo Bag* (1pt Background, req. Arete 1 — +1 die to clan affinity Sphere). Rote: *Loco's Palm* (Life 4, Mande only — heal 2 damage of any severity by sacrificing your own Stamina).

### Solificati / Children of Knowledge (pp. 102–109)
- **Concept:** Ancient alchemists ("**the Crown'd Ones**"), pursuers of the **Great Work** — elevating consciousness to immortality, not literal lead-into-gold. Defined by their fall: their member **Heylel Teomim** (a *rebis*, two-made-one) betrayed and was scattered by the Council of Nine. Reject the Hermetic "House Solificati" label. Vibrant, intense, often young-looking pranksters.
- **Paradigm/practice:** Alchemy as symbolic transformation ("everything is symbolic; alchemy isn't literal"). Euro-Arabian alchemy emphasizes **Matter/Life/Prime**; Asian (Taoist/Tantric) alchemy emphasizes **Prime/Forces/Spirit**; both use **Mind** and **Entropy** (permanence/decay). Practices: The Art of Desire, craftwork, dominion, elementalism, invigoration, medicine-work, weird science, hypertech, witchcraft, yoga.
- **Favored Spheres/foci:** **Matter, Prime, Life** (transmutation) plus Mind/Entropy; modern Solificati lean into psychotropics, rave/festival culture, and surreal art. Foci: symbolic artwork, brews/elixirs, drugs/poisons, cups/vessels, gems, formulae, computers/devices. Distinguish **instruments** (tools) from **Wonders** (enchanted items anyone can use).
- **Politics:** On-and-off outs with everyone — ejected from both Traditions and Technocracy historically. Friendly with the **Sons of Ether** (kindred mad scientists), the **Cult of Ecstasy/Akrites Solonikos** (party + the trickster-Hermes joke), less so the grudge-holding **Order of Hermes** and **Celestial Chorus**. Anti-Technocracy ("the Eternal Grinding Wheel"). Treat the Alliance as a useful temporary arrangement.
- **Hook:** A Neon Trickster (joyful rave-scene alchemist), an Occult Surrealist (near-mute painter of Quintessential art), a Street-Level Philanthropist (alchemy used to lift others from poverty).
- **Mechanics:** Alchemy focus through instruments; Wonders crafted per M20 pp. 651–653 / Book of Secrets pp. 139–159. Rotes: *Base/Complex/Sublime Transmutation* (Matter 2+, ±other Spheres — change one material to another; permanent gold is harder/draws attention, vulgar), *The White Queen's Kiss* (Mind 2/4 or Life 3/4 — induce sleep, scalable), *Elixir of Youth* (Life 3/4, ±Time 3 — slow aging, 5+ successes grants Unaging), *Wake the Red Lion* (Life 3/4 — temporarily raise an Attribute, 1 dot/success).

### Orphans (pp. 110–116)
- **Concept:** Self-taught lone mages with **no Tradition, Craft, or Convention** — the truly independent ("mages without Tradition"). Often clueless about the Ascension War (no one told them), fiercely self-reliant survivors. Not technically *members* of the Alliance (they're not a Craft), but useful allies. Some reclaim the term; old-school Orphans treat it as a slur.
- **Paradigm/practice:** Wholly personal/eclectic — whatever the individual believes. Survival ethic: stay low-profile, avoid Paradox, prefer **coincidental** non-flashy effects, don't draw the attention of Traditions ("Trads"), Technocracy ("Suits"), or worse. Build chosen family among Sleepers (women's shelters, queer communities, immigrants, religious charities).
- **Favored Spheres/foci:** None fixed — totally individual. Often lean on tech-magick, urban knowledge, street craft. Concepts trend modern: the Wild Talent (raw spontaneous Awakening), the Wintershiner (busy parent who started seeing ghosts), the App Developer (technomancer coder).
- **Politics:** Friend-adjacent with the **Disparates** generally; closest to the **Bata'a** and **Ngoma** (fellow survivors). Tolerated by the **Hollow Ones**. Wary of **Trads** (self-important "dickheads") and **Suits** (predatory). Believe the rumors of the **Fallen** (Nephandi) infiltrating both.
- **Hook:** Almost any lone newly-Awakened character; great for a PC discovering the wider world from scratch.
- **Mechanics:** *Technomancer / There's an App for That* (2–3pt Supernatural Merit — present magick as tech: halve Paradox from botching your highest Sphere's vulgar effect, unwitnessed at 2pt / witnessed at 4pt). Rotes: *I'm Good* (Life 2/3 — shake off illness), *Subway Map of the Supernatural* (Corr 2/Prime 1, ±Spirit/Life/Matter — locate other supernaturals on a city map), *Grapevine* (Corr 2/Mind 2, ±Time 2 — ask the ST general questions about the city/people), *Burn a Meal / Fool's Gold* (Mind 3 — convince an unAwakened person you paid them), *Revelation* (Life or Mind 3 — briefly let a Sleeper perceive reality as malleable).

## Overrides
This digest is an **expansion of character/faction options** only. It **does NOT override**:
- **Core resolution** — Storyteller d10 pool, difficulty 6 default, successes/botches, Willpower, specialties: all unchanged (use base M20 / `m20` subcommand).
- **The condition track** — Bruised→…→Incapacitated, bashing/lethal/aggravated, soak: unchanged.
- **The oracle** and progress clocks: unchanged.
- **Advancement/XP**: unchanged (use whatever XP rule is otherwise live; this book adds no new XP system).

What it **adds**: eleven playable Disparate Crafts as PC/NPC affiliations, each with faction-specific paradigms, favored Spheres/foci, Merits/Flaws, Backgrounds, and rotes (listed above). Templar **Orichalcum** uses the existing **Primium** rules (cosmetic/thematic difference only). Several rotes/wonders cross-reference *M20*, *M20: Book of Secrets*, *M20: How Do You Do That?*, and *M20: Gods & Monsters* (djinn, pp. 131–133) — consult those if a specific effect comes up in play.
