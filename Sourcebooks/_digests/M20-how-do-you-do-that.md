# Digest: Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary — *How Do You Do That?* (Sphere-effects expansion)

> Scan-fast adjudication reference for "**can I do X with my Spheres, and at what rank / cost?**" Organized by effect category, exactly as the book is. Each entry gives the common feat, the Sphere(s) and rough ranks, and whether it tends **vulgar** or **coincidental**. This is an **expansion**, not a rules replacement — it changes *nothing* about core resolution. See **Overrides**.

## System & source
- **Game & line:** Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary (M20)
- **Source:** *How Do You Do That? — A Practical Guide to Sphere Magick* (Onyx Path). An instructional appendix to M20, built from M20 Ch. 9–10; "when in doubt, default to **Mage 20, Chapter Ten**" (p. 13). Effect chapters: Conjuration/Transformation (pp. 18–25), Elemental Mastery (pp. 26–41), Energy-Work (pp. 42–51), Enhanced Perceptions (pp. 52–56), Martial Arts (pp. 57–69), Mystic Travel (pp. 70–83), Necromancy (pp. 84–89), Summoning/Binding/Bargaining/Warding (pp. 90–106), Time Distortion & Travel (pp. 107–113), Uncanny Influence (pp. 114–136). Front matter: Moments of Magick (pp. 15–17).
- **Dice subcommand:** `m20`

## Core resolution
**Unchanged from CLAUDE.md / M20 core. This book adds no new resolution mechanic.** For reference, magick uses:
- **Pool:** **Arete** dice (or, in extended/ritual casting, Arete rolled repeatedly), vs. a difficulty set by the highest Sphere used. Mundane "magick-enhanced" actions roll the normal **Attribute + Ability** pool with a Sphere lowering the difficulty (see *Magick Enhancing Abilities*, M20 p. 533).
- **Difficulty:** normally tied to the highest Sphere rank in the Effect, modified by coincidental/vulgar and circumstance. Standard d10 success counting; 1s cancel; botch = 1+ and zero successes.
- **Successes scale the feat:** most "how big / how far / how long" questions are answered by **success count**, read off a Feats chart (e.g. *Feats of Time Magick* p. 109, *Magickal Feats* chart M20 p. 502), not by rank alone. The book leans on the **Optional Dividing Successes Rule** (M20 Ch. 10) for splitting successes across magnitude vs. duration.
- **Willpower / specialties / extended rolls:** as core.

### Vulgar vs. coincidental — the through-line of this book
- **Coincidental** = the result *could* plausibly have happened without magick (a lock that was already loose, a "lucky" gust, a hunch, reading a mood). Most perception, social/Mind influence, subtle Life/chemistry, and small Forces nudges stay coincidental.
- **Vulgar** = reality visibly breaks (levitation, fireballs, teleport in view, shapeshifting, raising the dead, freezing time, instant healing of grievous wounds). Vulgar **with witnesses** is the worst Paradox tier.
- **Rules of thumb the book repeats:** *moving Time backward is ALWAYS vulgar* (p. 107); psychic *blasts/shatter* are vulgar (pp. 122); overt elemental conjuration/assault is vulgar; reading/nudging is coincidental; "obvious about using magick" flips an otherwise-coincidental feat to vulgar.

## Character rules
**Unchanged.** Sheet shape, creation, and **XP/advancement all stay as M20 core / CLAUDE.md** — this book defines no creation or advancement rules. It only tells you *what Spheres an effect needs and at what rank.*

## Key tables — effect → Sphere(s) / rank / vulgar-coincidental

## On "moments of magick," combining Spheres, optional rules (pp. 15–17, 504, 509)
- **A "moment of magick"** is one Effect = one Arete roll; you choose the Spheres, the book tells you the floor ranks above. **Multiple Spheres stack into one Effect** at the highest rank's difficulty — the higher/more Spheres, the harder and more vulgar.
- **"A Bit of Spirit?" (p. 16):** optional rule letting a Background-bonded spirit lend an Effect (e.g. a Wolf-totem shaman channeling Life via the bond). Off by default; ST may allow.
- **The book repeatedly defers to optional M20 rules** it assumes are *available, not mandatory:* **Optional Dividing Successes Rule** (split successes between magnitude and duration), **Thresholds** (M20 p. 387, extra success per +1 over diff 9), and **The Difficulties of Going Back in Time** (p. 111, time-reversal +3 diff). Treat each as off unless the table opts in.
- **"My Character's Better at This Than I Am" (p. 119):** social feats — roll the trait, narrate *why* it's persuasive; ST may bonus clever play / penalize lame play, within normal modifier bounds. Guidance, not a mechanic.

## Overrides
**None. This digest overrides nothing.** *How Do You Do That?* is an **expansion** that adds *effect-adjudication guidance only* — what Spheres/ranks a given feat needs and whether it reads vulgar or coincidental. It **does not change**:
- **Core resolution** (Arete pools, d10 success counting, difficulty, botches) — unchanged from CLAUDE.md / M20.
- **The condition track / Health levels / soak** — unchanged.
- **Advancement / XP** — unchanged.
- Any creation rules.

The only "rules" it introduces are **optional** sub-systems (Dividing Successes, Thresholds, Going-Back-in-Time +3, "A Bit of Spirit?") that are **off unless the table turns them on**. When live, note this digest in `Game/system.md` purely as an effects reference.
