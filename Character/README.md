# Character

Your character lives here. During Session Zero the Game Master (with the **character-creator**) fills in:

- **`sheet.md`** — your character's traits, edges, troubles, bonds, gear, and condition track. This is a *living* sheet: it changes as you grow, get hurt, gain items, or level up.
- **`backstory.md`** — who you are and where you came from.
- **`portraits/`** — drop in any images of your character you like. (The GM can't see them as art, but you can, and they help you stay immersed.)

Nothing here is secret from you — it's *your* character.
