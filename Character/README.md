# Character

Your character lives here. During Session Zero the Storyteller (with the **character-creator**) fills in your **Mage: The Ascension** character:

- **`sheet.md`** — your character's Attributes, Abilities, Backgrounds, Willpower, Health levels, Merits/Flaws, and the Mage traits (the nine Spheres, Arete, Quintessence/Paradox, Avatar, and your paradigm, practice & instruments). This is a *living* sheet: it changes as you grow, get hurt, gain dots, or spend experience to advance (see the **Advancement** section — your visible XP ledger).
- **`backstory.md`** — who you are and where you came from.
- **`portraits/`** — drop in any images of your character you like. (The GM can't see them as art, but you can, and they help you stay immersed.)

Nothing here is secret from you — it's *your* character.
