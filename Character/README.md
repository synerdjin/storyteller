# Character

Your character lives here. During Session Zero the Storyteller (with the **character-creator**) fills in, in the shape of your chosen World of Darkness game (M20 / V20 / W20):

- **`sheet.md`** — your character's Attributes, Abilities, Backgrounds, Willpower, Health levels, Merits/Flaws, and the splat traits of your game (Spheres & Arete, Disciplines & Humanity, or Gifts & Renown). This is a *living* sheet: it changes as you grow, get hurt, gain dots, or spend experience to advance (see the **Advancement** section — your visible XP ledger).
- **`backstory.md`** — who you are and where you came from.
- **`portraits/`** — drop in any images of your character you like. (The GM can't see them as art, but you can, and they help you stay immersed.)

Nothing here is secret from you — it's *your* character.
