# <Name> — SECRETS (GM ONLY)

> ⚠️ Never hand this file to the `npc-actor`. Never quote it to the Player. This is what the GM knows about this character that the character hides — or doesn't even know themselves.

## The truth
- Who or what they really are; what they're really after.

## What they're hiding
- The lies they tell, the things they conceal, and the tell that might give them away under pressure.

## Reveal conditions
- What would expose this, and what should happen when it does.

## Knowledge they have that the player doesn't
- Secret facts about the plot or world this character is aware of, so the GM can have them act on it without ever stating it aloud.
