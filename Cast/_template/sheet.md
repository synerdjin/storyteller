## Health levels
- **Bruised → Hurt → Injured → Wounded → Mauled → Crippled → Incapacitated** — mark the current level as harm lands; apply the wound penalty to their pools.
- Current: **Bruised** (unharmed)
- **Soak / damage types:** what they soak easily and what's **aggravated** against them (fire, raw Quintessence, soulfire, the worst Paradox).

## Supernatural & levers
- **Powers worth tracking:** the Spheres they command and a signature effect or two, Arete, Quintessence/Paradox on hand — or, for a Sleeper or hedge-wizard, whatever passes for power — only what will actually come up.
- **The lever the Player can exploit** — a Flaw, a Quiet, a brittle paradigm, a Paradox risk, a divided loyalty — to gain advantage or turn the scene.

## Tactics
- Signature effects, rotes, weapons, or wonders.
- **How they fight or scheme to win** — play the opposition honestly (see CLAUDE.md). What do they *do* when threatened, and what makes them break off, flee, frenzy, or parley?
