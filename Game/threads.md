# Threads — the Player's thread view

> Open questions, quests, mysteries, promises, and foreshadowing **that the Player's character actually knows about or has touched.** Add threads as they arise; mark them resolved (don't delete) when they pay off, so the history stays readable.
>
> This is the **player-facing view** of `Game/plots.md` (the GM-only master registry of *all* plots, including the hidden and off-screen ones). Keep it strictly derived: a plot appears here only once the Player's involvement reaches `aware`/`observing`/`participating`. The world plots the Player has never heard of stay in `plots.md` only.
