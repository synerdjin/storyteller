# Boundaries & tone

> Set during Session Zero, before anything else. The Game Master honors this absolutely — no exceptions. Update it any time; the GM adapts immediately.

## Tone & genre
- **Genre:**
- **Mood:** (e.g. hopeful adventure, grim survival, cozy mystery, pulpy heroics …)
- **Rating:** how dark / violent / mature you want things to get.
- **Pace:** more action, more roleplay & intrigue, more exploration?

## Lines — never include
Things you do **not** want in the story at all. The GM steers completely clear.
-

## Veils — keep off-screen
Things that can exist in the story but should **fade to black** rather than be depicted.
-

## Anything else
- Tropes you love or hate, content notes, how hard you want to be challenged, lethality (can the character die?), and so on.
