# Developments — the world's pending moves (GM ONLY)

> ⚠️ GM-only staging area. The `world-director` writes here after each tick: what the living world did off-screen, and *when* each thing should reach the Player. The GM drains it — weaving "surface now" items into the next scene as live pressure, holding the rest. **Never** quoted wholesale to the Player; it's a backstage queue, not a newspaper.
>
> Empty until the living world starts moving. Append newest at the top. Mark an entry **drained** (don't delete) once you've worked it into play, so you keep a record.

### Entry format (for the director)
```
## [Day N — in-world date] — <agent>: <one-line headline>
- What happened (off-screen).
- Surface: now | soon (trigger: ...) | hidden
- Drained: no
```
- **now** — the Player can perceive this; give it to them as pressure in the next scene.
- **soon** — surfaces when the named trigger fires (they visit the docks, the festival arrives).
- **hidden** — consequences are brewing but not yet perceptible; it ripens toward a later reveal.
